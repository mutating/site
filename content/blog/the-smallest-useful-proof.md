title: "The smallest useful proof"
date: 2026-06-24T09:00:00+03:00
description: "Evidence is strongest when it is minimal enough to inspect and complete enough to act on."
tags: [proof, evidence]

Large logs contain information but often conceal the reason a judgment was made.

## Reduce without erasing

Keep the smallest reproduction that still preserves the violated invariant and its causal context.
