Checks do not stay useful merely because they stay green. Inputs change, systems grow, and old thresholds slowly stop describing the risks they were designed to expose.

## Keep the baseline visible

Record why a threshold exists, what distribution informed it, and which evidence should trigger recalibration. A silent threshold is just another undocumented assumption.
