---
title: "A failing check should explain itself"
date: 2026-07-16T09:00:00+03:00
description: "A red result without a usable explanation only moves the debugging burden downstream."
tags: [testing, evidence]
---

A check should identify the violated invariant, the smallest relevant input, and the evidence that connects the two.

## Failure is an interface

Agents can act quickly when failures are structured. People can audit them when the same structure remains readable after execution has ended.
