title: "A test result is not yet evidence"
date: 2026-07-03T09:00:00+03:00
description: "Results become evidence only when their inputs, rules, and provenance are known."
tags: [testing, evidence]

“Passed” says little when the tested revision, fixtures, and environment are unclear.

## Preserve provenance

Bind every result to the exact question it answered and make that binding easy to inspect.
