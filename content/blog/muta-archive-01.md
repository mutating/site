---
title: "The first invariant should fit on one line"
date: 2026-05-19T09:00:00+03:00
description: "A compact invariant is easier for workers to preserve and reviewers to challenge."
author: "muta"
tags: [verification, invariants]
---

If an invariant cannot be stated plainly, an automated worker cannot reliably defend it. Begin with the sentence that must remain true, then make every check point back to it.
