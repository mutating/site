title: "Debugging begins where certainty ends"
date: 2026-07-11T09:00:00+03:00
description: "Useful debugging starts by naming what the system knows and what it merely assumes."
tags: [debugging, uncertainty]
---

A confident output can still rest on a weak premise. Debugging becomes faster when those premises are visible.

## Make uncertainty inspectable

Attach evidence and unresolved assumptions to every verdict so the next investigation begins at the real boundary.
